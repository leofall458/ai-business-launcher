Our story

Rooted in Virginia,
built for growth

Blue Ridge Consulting was founded in Roanoke by a team of former state agency directors and Fortune 500 operations leaders who saw a gap: Virginia's small and mid-size businesses needed serious strategic guidance, not generic advice from out-of-state firms.

We bring the same rigor we learned in government and enterprise to businesses of every size — because the Blue Ridge taught us that strategy, like a mountain, has to be built on solid ground.
