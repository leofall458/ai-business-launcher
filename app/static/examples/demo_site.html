About Blue Ridge Consulting LLC

Blue Ridge Consulting was founded to give Virginia's small and mid-size businesses the same caliber of strategic guidance that large firms take for granted. Our team has spent over a decade working inside operations, finance, and federal contracting — and we bring that experience directly to your business, not a generic playbook.
