Privacy Policy

Launch Bridge LLC · Last updated June 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the information you provide when using our LLC formation service, including your name, email address, phone number, mailing address, business information, and Social Security Number (SSN) for the purpose of filing your EIN application with the IRS.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely to:

  • File your Virginia LLC Articles of Organization with the Virginia State Corporation Commission
  • Apply for your EIN with the IRS on your behalf
  • Generate your business formation documents
  • Communicate with you about your order

3. Social Security Number

Your SSN is collected solely for the purpose of completing IRS Form SS-4 (EIN application).

  • Your Social Security Number is encrypted using Google Cloud KMS (AES-256) before storage.
  • Your SSN is automatically deleted from our systems within 72 hours or immediately after your EIN is issued, whichever comes first.
  • Your SSN is never transmitted to or processed by any AI system, including Google Gemini.
  • Google Cloud Platform serves as our infrastructure sub-processor under Google's Data Processing Agreement.
  • You may request immediate deletion of your SSN by contacting support@launchbridge.ai.

4. Data Storage

Your business information (excluding SSN) is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore. We do not sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties except as required to complete your filing (Virginia SCC, IRS).

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and secure cloud infrastructure. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

6. Contact Us

For privacy questions contact us at: support@launchbridge.ai
